# Roadmap

1. Soybean model inventory and literature review; establish model and observation availability.
2. Define one feasible yield evaluation task and preregister comparison protocol.
3. Implement a minimal reproducible pilot with simple baselines and eligible crop models.
4. Evaluate on held-out cases, assess uncertainty and robustness, and prepare a reviewed report.
5. Extract reusable evaluation utilities after the pilot. Expand to maize, wheat, weather, hydrology or energy only when a concrete question and sufficient resources exist.

Every item is planned; no model execution or result is claimed.
